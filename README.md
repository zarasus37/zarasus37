## Lumen Grove

A mobile arcade-defense hybrid — and the **balance harness** that proves its
difficulty curve is right.

🎮 **Play it free, no signup** → https://hy5uvm1q5ywq8.space.minimax.io
📄 **The method (free)** → https://github.com/zarasus37/lumen-grove/blob/main/docs/HARNESS.md
🛠 **Full source licence** → https://gumroad.com/l/lumen-grove

The simulation imports nothing from Phaser, so the whole game runs headless —
which is what lets you *sweep* a difficulty constant instead of guessing at it:

```
  value | careful | no-tend | gappy | max careful | max gappy | sparks
  ------+---------+---------+-------+-------------+------------+--------
  1.27  | 11n      | 6n      | 5n    | 14n         | 7n         | 12
  1.42  | 8n       | 4n      | 4n    | 13n         | 6n         | 6
```

The rule it enforces: careful play must clearly beat naive play. If it stops
doing that, your upgrade mechanic is decoration.
